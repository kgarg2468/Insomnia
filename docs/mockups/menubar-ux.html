What goes away

The preset popover, the session popover, the “?” badges, the “Enter starts · Esc closes” hint, the frosted-glass materials and blur glows inside the status item. Presets are gone too: typing 4 Enter is two keystrokes, faster than aiming at a chip. Settings and Quit move to a right-click menu, which is a native NSMenu and cannot be dismissed by its own clicks.

This mockup only imitates the look and the interactions. Sleep, lid actions and recovery are not simulated here; see the README for what the real app does and what it cannot guarantee.
